# Sample Trade Requests (AI Test Inputs)

This document contains realistic trade client requests used for:
- AI testing
- demo scenarios
- relevance validation

These are **inputs only** (what designers say), not expected responses.

---

## General Sourcing Requests

1. “I’m looking for an 8x10 for a living room — neutral, nothing too busy. Budget is flexible.”
2. “Do you have any runners that would work in a hallway? Something durable, not too thick.”
3. “Client wants something warm and soft, more texture than pattern.”
4. “Looking for a statement rug for a formal living room. Traditional but not overly ornate.”
5. “Do you have anything vintage-feeling but lighter in palette?”

---

## Style-Specific Requests

6. “Any Oushaks around 9x12? Muted tones, preferably.”
7. “Looking for a Kazak or Heriz style, bold but not distressed.”
8. “Client loves Moroccan rugs — black and ivory preferred.”
9. “Do you have any flatweaves or kilims that could work in a modern space?”
10. “Something Persian-inspired but not too red.”

---

## Budget & Constraint-Based Requests

11. “Need to stay under $6k if possible.”
12. “This is for staging, so I’m looking for something affordable and neutral.”
13. “Client has pets — nothing too delicate.”
14. “Need something that won’t shed much.”
15. “This is for a kid’s room, so easy care is important.”

---

## Size & Room Context

16. “Primary bedroom rug, 9x12, should feel calm and soft.”
17. “Looking for a rug to anchor a sectional — probably 10x14.”
18. “Do you have anything narrow that could work as a kitchen runner?”
19. “Entryway rug — needs to be durable but still nice.”
20. “Office rug, not too thick so chairs can roll.”

---

## Follow-Ups & Iteration

21. “Can you show me a few options similar to the last one you sent?”
22. “Anything a little lighter than those?”
23. “Do you have something with less contrast?”
24. “Can we look at one step up in quality?”
25. “Client wants to see alternatives before deciding.”

---

## Availability & Logistics

26. “Is that piece still available?”
27. “Can you hold this for me until tomorrow?”
28. “How quickly could this be ready for pickup or delivery?”
29. “Do you have anything similar in stock right now?”
30. “If this sells, do you have backups I could show?”

---

## Appraisal-Adjacent Requests

31. “Client wants an appraisal included with the rug.”
32. “Can you generate an appraisal for insurance purposes?”
33. “They’re asking for documentation on the rug’s value.”
34. “We’ll need something professional-looking for the client file.”

---

## Relationship-Based / Casual Requests

35. “Hey — working on another project, thought I’d check what’s new.”
36. “Client loved the last rugs you found — looking for something similar.”
37. “Trust your judgment on this one — just send your best picks.”
38. “Need a few strong options to present.”
39. “Anything you think would work well here?”
40. “Let me know if something special comes in.”

---

## Design Intent

These requests are intentionally:
- informal
- incomplete
- real-world
- varied in specificity

The AI should:
- ask clarifying questions when needed
- use stored preferences when available
- avoid assumptions
- feel like a knowledgeable rug specialist, not a chatbot
